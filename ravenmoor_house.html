Agency case file 31-A · Oct 30, 1998 · 11:41 PM

RavenmoorHouse

A two-agent light-gun night. Shoot the dead, save the living, and reload by firing off the screen.

For the friend who always stood at the other gun.

Controls

Agent 1
Mouse to aim, left click to fire. Right click, or click outside the screen, to reload.
Agent 2
WASD or arrows to aim (hold Shift for fine aim), Space to fire, R to reload.
Gamepad
Stick to aim, A or RT fire, X or LT reload. Plays as Agent 2.
Touch
Tap to fire, RELOAD button or a second finger to reload.
Other
P or Esc pauses, M mutes. Agent 2 can join any time by firing.

Field notes

  • Headshots drop them fast. Shoot off an arm to slow the grab.
  • Shoot thrown axes and fireballs out of the air.
  • Never shoot survivors. Saving them opens safer routes.
  • Break crates and vases, then shoot the item to grab it.
  • Bosses only bleed at the weak point.
