Keep Onslaught

A castle-defense tribute to the 2009 iPhone classic Knights Onrush

  • Grab a knight with your finger and fling him. A high drop or a hard slam into the wall finishes him.
  • Throw a knight into his friends to knock them all down.
  • Buy the Dragon Head, then carry knights up to its jaws to feed it. Each one fed pays double gold.
  • Battering rams are too heavy to lift. Drop boulders and anvils on them, or pour boiling oil.
  • Tap a wizard's magic bubble to pop it.
  • Between waves, spend gold on traps, archers and repairs.
