Keep Onslaught

A castle-defense tribute to the 2009 iPhone classic Knights Onrush

  • Grab a knight and fling him. A high drop or a slam into the wall finishes him.
  • Buy the Hell Gate fire pit, then throw knights into it to burn them and collect their souls.
  • Buy the Dragon Altar, then hang a knight on its hook. The dragon swoops in to eat him.
  • Spend gold and souls in the Castle Shop on doors, boulders, columns, fireballs and guns.
