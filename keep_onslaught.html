Keep Onslaught

Inspired by the castle-defense games I loved on the early iPhone

  • Grab a knight and fling him. A high drop or a slam into the wall finishes him.
  • Buy the Ember Pit, then throw knights into it to burn them and free their spirits.
  • Raise the Worm Spire, then hang a knight on its hook. The Worm swoops in to eat him.
  • Spend gold and spirits on the Keeper's Goods: stronger gates, falling stones, pillars, fire and battlement engines.
