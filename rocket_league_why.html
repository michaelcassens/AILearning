How It Works

The four tiers are a thinking ladder, not a rank ladder

Every question in the bank is tagged to a tier. The tier is not "how good you are at Rocket League" — it is what kind of cognitive work the question demands. A Diamond who has never questioned a habit can be stuck at Tier 1 thinking, and a Gold who reasons carefully can operate at Tier 2.

The four tiers of analytical demand
TierDemandThe question sounds like
1 — IdentifyName what happened, and separate what you saw from what you concluded."What actually went wrong here, as opposed to what I felt went wrong?"
2 — ExplainTrace the cause through the systems of the game: boost, space, time, rotation."What made that situation unwinnable eight seconds before the shot?"
3 — EvaluateJudge a decision on the information available at the time, not on the result."Given what I could perceive at that moment, was there a better option — and could I have executed it?"
4 — ModelReason about your own reasoning, and about the opponent reasoning about you."What is my model of this player, how would I know if it's wrong, and what does he think I'm about to do?"

How the engine adapts

  • Each of the six tracks has its own depth value between 1.0 and 4.0. Answering well pushes that track's depth up; missing pulls it down. Depth moves faster on a miss than on a hit, which keeps you near the edge of what you can actually do.
  • The engine serves the weakest unmastered track most often, so you spend your time where the return is highest, with occasional questions from stronger tracks so they do not decay.
  • Mastery is capped by evidence. Reaching Proficient in a track requires correct answers at Tier 3 or above. Mastered requires Tier 4. Grinding Tier 1 questions will plateau you at Familiar, which is the honest result.
  • Hints are a ladder: the first hint reframes the question, the second narrows the field, the third all but answers it. Using hints reduces the credit for that item, because a hinted answer is weaker evidence that you can do it unaided.

What this module cannot do

There is no AI grading your written answers here — this page is a single HTML file with no server and no model behind it. Free-response items are scored by an explicit rubric that you apply to your own answer after seeing an expert response. That is a real technique (self-explanation with a worked example), and it works, but it depends on your honesty. Every free-response item also has a Copy coaching prompt button that packages your answer, the scenario, and your current depth into a prompt you can paste into an AI assistant that can read and respond to it.
